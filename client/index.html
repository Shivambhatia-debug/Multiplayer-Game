        How to survive

        • The reactor is the heart of the colony. If it hits 0%, the game is over. It repairs slowly between waves.
        • Energy is shared. Walk into glowing blue power cells, kill aliens and clear waves to earn it.
        • Auto Turrets shoot on their own. Generators make energy. Med Stations heal you. Barricades hold aliens back.
        • The Xal: Drones are fast, Brutes are tanks, Spitters shoot acid from range. Shoot drop pods before they land or they release more drones.
        • The compass and radar show aliens (green), pods (red), power cells (blue) and the reactor (gold).
