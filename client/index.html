    Mission brief

    1. Warm it. Thermal Cores push the planet above freezing.
    2. Power it. Solar Pylons feed every machine. Night means less power.
    3. Breathe & drink. Scrubbers clean the air, Condensers fill the oceans.
    4. Seed it. Trees spread on their own once conditions are right.
    5. Defend it. Shoot meteors and the blight crawlers that come to eat your base.

    Hold the biosphere above 90% for 12 seconds before the colony ship arrives (16:00).

      The simulation

      • Energy is shared. Walk into cyan crystals to mine it. Pylons produce more on the day side.
      • Heat must sit between 45 and 65 (≈10–28°C). Condensers stay idle while the planet is frozen.
      • Water rises as a real ocean. Low land floods, so build high.
      • Trees breathe out oxygen and spread, but drown or burn when things go wrong.
      • Meteors wipe out everything inside their red ring, yet they bring heat and ice.
      • Blight crawlers walk to your machines and eat them, and they poison the air. Two shots kill one; big brutes take five.
      • Bounties pay big energy rewards. The radar (bottom left) shows crawlers in purple and meteors in red.
      • Stabilize the planet before the colony ship arrives, or the mission fails.
